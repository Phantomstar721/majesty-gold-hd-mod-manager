# Majesty Mod Manager 0.3.8

## What's new

- Support for GOG Gold HD 1.5.2.28, including already-downloaded Workshop content and the bundled quality-of-life options.
- Easier switching between supported game installations.
- Fixes for crashes when opening custom hero ability panels and missing hover descriptions.
- Research effects now appear on the building that completed the research.
- Support for percentage-based movement effects, with bonuses adding together when different effects overlap.
- Shared list, recruitment, and research-panel improvements, including lower-resolution layouts.
- Improvements to combining compatible scripts, descriptions, and artwork.

Some advanced mod features, including the new movement effects, currently
require Steam beta2. The Manager checks compatibility with your selected
installation. GOG 1.5.1.2 is not supported, and the Manager does not download
Workshop subscriptions.

## Updating

Close Majesty and the Manager. Extract the complete ZIP to your Manager folder;
keep `Majesty Mod Manager.exe` beside its `_internal` folder. Workshop users
should let Steam finish downloading the update instead.

Update your selected mods, reopen the Manager, **Rescan Content**, then
**Prepare Selected Mods** (or **Prepare Again**) before **Launch Majesty**.
Standard mods continue using Majesty's normal load order.
