# Majesty Mod Manager 0.3.7

## Changes

- Multi-row list panels, optional rewards, parent actions, and stock single-panel behavior at lower resolutions.
- Shared hero-task integration and gameplay notifications, including attack-flag completion independently of gold payout.
- Opt-in private hero participation and three-choice recruitment panels with stock progress, prices, and small-font support.
- Package-owned artwork for stock-shaped AP10 toggle buttons, with exact state/frame validation and no custom controller.
- Independent edits within shared GPL functions and XML Descriptions can combine; competing edits still stop with conflict details.
- More precise art ancestry and inherited tile handling to prevent unrelated images from sharing the wrong art source.
- Opt-in native timing queries and stronger agreement checks between prepared feature data and launch requirements.

## Updating

Close Majesty and the Manager before updating. Keep the EXE with its complete
`_internal` folder. After updating your selected mods, open the Manager,
**Rescan Content**, **Prepare Selected Mods** (or **Prepare Again**), and
**Launch Majesty**. Standard mods continue using Majesty's normal load order;
this update does not fold them into the Merge package.
