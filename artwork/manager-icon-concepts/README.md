# Majesty Mod Manager icon concepts

These five concepts were generated independently with OpenAI's built-in image
generation tool. **Royal Scribe** was selected and is now wired into the
application; the other four remain review alternatives.

All prompts shared this intent: an original Windows taskbar/title-bar icon for
a Majesty-themed mod manager, painted like a late-1990s fantasy strategy-game
interface asset, transparent, readable at 16x16 and 32x32, without text,
letters, an existing logo, or a watermark.

## 1. Royal Scribe

> A bold golden quill crossing an unfurled parchment, with three simple
> burgundy wax seals aligned on the parchment to suggest organizing several
> mods. Aged parchment, antique gold, deep burgundy, dark-brown outline. No
> shield, gear, crown, or scenery.

## 2. Arcane Convergence

> A crystal orb on a small antique-gold wizard stand; inside the orb, three
> large glowing rune-stones converge into one bright central point, suggesting
> multiple mods becoming one. Smoky violet glass, warm gold, parchment-white
> glow, restrained teal accent. No shield, gear, crown, character, or scenery.

## 3. Builder's Keep

> A compact fantasy castle gate with three distinct small tower blocks joining
> into one central keep; the raised portcullis forms a simple downward chevron,
> suggesting assembling a kingdom from modular pieces. Warm sandstone, aged
> gold, forest-green banners. No shield, gear, crown, people, or scenery.

## 4. Royal Mod Chest

> A small medieval treasure chest opened just enough to reveal three neatly
> stacked glowing scroll-cases, suggesting a curated collection of game mods;
> one gold clasp shaped like three joining links. Dark walnut, antique gold,
> parchment glow, royal-red lining. No shield, gear, crown, coins, weapons, or
> scenery.

## 5. Royal Astrolabe

> A circular medieval astrolabe or royal compass: three broad gold pointers
> from different directions lock into one central jewel, suggesting selecting
> and combining several mods into one launch. Antique gold, dark burgundy,
> parchment-white highlight, charcoal edge. No shield, gear, crown, character,
> scenery, tiny tick marks, or filigree.
